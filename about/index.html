About Spooder

Spooder is an iPhone app for finding your friends and the events happening around you. It's coming!

Who makes it

Spooder is built by Spooder LLC, a limited liability company organized in Pennsylvania. Spooder LLC owns and operates this website and the Spooder app, and it will be the publisher of record on the App Store. Nicholas Coleman is the sole developer.

Why it exists

I love authentication, but I hate Single Sign On!

Out: Login experiences that bounce you around, password managers that get hacked, codes sent to your phone, and single sign on that makes you sign on more than a single time

In: You don't need an account, you don't need to login, your identity is your device, and you can use FaceId or TouchId if your device changes.

Why "Spooder"

While I was building this thing, a Zebra jumping spider moved into my office. He hung around for weeks, then moseyed to the kitchen window. I named him our Chief Financial Officer. We're going all in on spiders, plus the domain was cheap.

He has since passed away, but he was avenged.

Where things stand

The app is in active development and will be released on the App Store for iPhone. Updates will be posted here on spooder.app as launch gets closer.
